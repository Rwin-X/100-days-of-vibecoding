Form Fundamentals
Running Technique
Five principles. None of them forced — all of them natural, given time.
Posture
Head neutral. Eyes forward. Shoulders relaxed.
Foot Strike
Do not force a specific foot strike. Aim for a comfortable, natural landing beneath the body.
Arms
Elbows at approximately 90°. Move arms forward and backward rather than excessively across the body.
Cadence
Do not force an arbitrary cadence number. Let cadence develop naturally with comfortable form and pace.
Breathing
Use relaxed, rhythmic breathing. Easy running should feel controlled rather than breathless.
